The **Lagrange ZK Coprocessor** is currently deployed as an Active Validation Service (AVS) on EigenLayer across both **Holesky testnet** and **Ethereum mainnet** networks. These deployments enable operators to stake their ETH and provide proving services for verifiable blockchain computations, with the mainnet deployment supporting production workloads and the Holesky deployment serving as a testing environment for new features and operator onboarding.
