## What is the Lagrange ZK Prover Network?

The **Lagrange ZK Prover Network (LPN)** is a modular, infinitely scalable foundational proving layer designed to unlock universal proof generation for a variety of use cases, such as rollups, ZK Coprocessor, DApps, and interoperability at internet scale.

### Key Features

- **EigenLayer Integration**: Deployed on EigenLayer and supported by over 85 top institution-grade operators including Coinbase, OKX, P2P, Nethermind, and others
- **Modular Architecture**: Each operator runs multiple provers to deliver ZK proofs requested by users
- **Subnetwork Support**: Multiple independent subnetworks with dedicated bandwidth that collectively form a "prover network of prover networks"
- **Customizable Standards**: Any blockchain, rollup, or application can connect through customizable standards and scale without bottlenecks or I/O constraints

## Architecture Benefits

The ZK Prover Network supports multiple independent subnetworks with dedicated bandwidth that collectively form a "prover network of prover networks". This approach allows any blockchain, rollup, or application to connect through customizable standards and scale without bottlenecks or I/O constraints.

**This model guarantees:**

- **High liveness** and availability
- **Simple interface** for integration
- **Support for complex proof generation** required for large ecosystems

## Problems Solved

Lagrange ZK Prover Network addresses key challenges experienced by rollups and applications:

### **Scalability Issues**

- Eliminates single-threaded bottlenecks
- Enables seamless handling of thousands of proof requests simultaneously
- Maintains performance even during peak demand

### **Availability Concerns**

- Removes single points of failure through decentralized architecture
- Distributes proof generation across a network of operators
- Ensures consistent service availability

### **High Proving Costs**

- Reduces operational expenses through competitive marketplace dynamics
- Optimizes resource allocation across the network

### **Proof Computation Latencies**

- Minimizes delays through efficient job distribution
- Leverages parallel processing capabilities
- Provides predictable performance metrics

## Censorship Resistance & Decentralization

Lagrange's ZK Prover Network eliminates censorship and liveness risks by distributing proof generation across a decentralized network of operators. This design ensures seamless handling of thousands of proof requests simultaneously, even during peak demand, without reliance on a single point of failure.

Together, these capabilities empower builders to scale with confidence, knowing that proof generation will never become a bottleneck.
